My Decision-Making Approach

I believe strong marketing decisions come from understanding the problem before immediately changing the solution.

Define → Investigate → Analyze → Evaluate Risk → Recommend → Measure → Improve

I also evaluate decisions by asking three questions:

What is the potential upside?
How much could the business gain if the decision is correct?

What is the downside?
How much could the business lose if the decision is wrong?

Can the decision be reversed?
If it can, we can move faster. If it cannot, we slow down and gather stronger evidence.

Marketing Budget Optimization

Evaluating multi-channel marketing performance and recommending strategic budget allocation under financial constraints.

Independent Portfolio Case Study — Simulated Business Scenario

This project was created as part of my professional development to demonstrate marketing analysis, performance evaluation, budget allocation, and evidence-based decision-making. It does not represent work completed for a real client.

Business Situation

A growing e-commerce company was investing in several digital marketing channels but was not achieving consistent returns across those channels.

Leadership wanted to determine how the existing marketing budget could be allocated more efficiently while maintaining sufficient reach and customer acquisition.

Business Objective

The primary objective was to identify which marketing channels were generating the strongest financial performance and recommend a more efficient allocation of the available budget.

  • Improve return on advertising spend (ROAS).
  • Reduce inefficient marketing expenditure.
  • Protect high-performing acquisition channels.
  • Develop a data-driven budget allocation strategy.

Available Marketing Data

Finding 1 — Email Marketing

Email marketing generated the highest return on investment in the dataset, producing approximately $5 in revenue for every $1 spent. This suggests that the channel should be protected and potentially expanded where additional capacity exists.

Finding 2 — Paid Search

Paid Search demonstrated strong performance with a 3.5x ROAS. Because the channel already produces substantial revenue, it represents a strong candidate for maintaining or moderately increasing investment.

Finding 3 — Display Advertising

Display advertising produced the lowest ROAS at 1.5x. Rather than automatically eliminating the channel, the appropriate response would be to investigate audience targeting, creative performance, placement quality, and conversion behavior before making a final decision.

Recommended Budget Allocation

Based on the simulated performance data, I would shift budget away from lower-performing activity and increase investment in channels demonstrating stronger returns.

Channel Current Budget Recommended Budget Action
Paid Search $12,000 $14,000 Increase moderately
Paid Social $10,000 $9,000 Optimize
Display Advertising $8,000 $5,000 Reduce and investigate
Email Marketing $3,000 $5,000 Increase

Strategic Recommendation

The recommended strategy is not simply to spend more money. It is to allocate additional budget toward channels with stronger demonstrated performance while using controlled testing to improve or validate weaker channels.

The largest opportunity appears to be expanding email marketing and maintaining strong Paid Search performance while reducing exposure to inefficient Display spending.

Measurement Plan

After implementing the new allocation, performance should be monitored using a consistent measurement framework.

  • ROAS by channel
  • Cost per acquisition (CPA)
  • Conversion rate
  • Revenue generated
  • Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
  • Return on marketing investment

Key Takeaway

Effective budget optimization requires more than identifying the channel with the highest ROAS. A strong marketer considers performance, scalability, customer behavior, risk, and the quality of available evidence before reallocating budget.
